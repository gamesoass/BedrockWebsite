Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy covers bedrockcalculator.pro and its games, including GLOOP.io at gloop.bedrockcalculator.pro.

What we collect

You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your email or real name. In multiplayer games, the nickname and colour you pick and your in-game position are shared live with other players while you play. They are not stored after you leave. Your browser may remember your nickname and colour (local storage) so you don't have to type them again.

Server logs

Like most websites, our server and hosting providers may briefly process technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages, keep games running and prevent abuse.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
